About Us

H4 Networks LLC is a telecom contracting company providing design, deployment, and analysis services for wireless and wired network infrastructure. We work with carriers, ISPs, and enterprise clients to build and optimize communications networks.

Services

FTTH

Fiber-to-the-home network design, splicing, and installation for residential and commercial deployments.

LTE

LTE site build-out, equipment installation, and network commissioning support.

LoRa

LoRaWAN gateway deployment and network planning for IoT connectivity.

RF Analysis

RF site surveys, spectrum analysis, and coverage/interference assessments.
